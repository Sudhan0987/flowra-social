# Standing rules from Sudhan's feedback

Every stage of the social agent reads this file before it works (research,
story, storyboard, director, final test). Each rule says what to do and why.
When new feedback arrives, add it to `log.md` with the date, then turn it into
a rule here. Never break a rule here to follow a generic best practice.

## Styles
- **Never the same style twice in a row.** Reels alternate between styles (brand/styles/styles.json): Floey's garden, and a professional explainer (a narrator over a drawn working screen: charts, tables, a cursor clicking, in useflowra.com's colours). Why (12 Oct 2026): "dont make the reels in same style man... we are repeating the same style over and over... make some professional reels also."
- **The rules under "Characters and world" are for Floey's garden reels.** A professional reel has no mascot in the story (Floey only perches on the logo in its end card) and follows brand/styles/pro/README.md. Every other rule here applies to every style.

## Characters and world (Floey's garden reels)
- **Floey is the only character.** A garden reel is told through Floey the hummingbird (brand/mascot) in her page-flower garden (brand/world). No generic stick figures, robots or new mascots. Why: the stick-figure reels were rejected as generic; Floey was loved.
- **Floey eats bugs.** Website problems (404s, broken links, errors) are little bugs; Floey catches them mid-air and eats them, like real hummingbirds eat insects. Use this when a reel is about errors or fixes. Why: Sudhan's brother's idea; it is funny and true to the animal.
- **Pages are flowers.** Health shows in the flower: fading pages lose colour, droop and yellow; thriving pages are bright, glow and sway. If the voice says a page is fading, the flower must visibly fade too.
- **The background has depth but never competes.** Keep the sun, clouds, mountains and hills pale and soft; Floey and the flowers stay the brightest things. Why: the plain background read as empty.

## Story and script
- **Talk to the viewer.** Say "your site", "your page". Never "a site owner", "a marketer" or a stranger's story. Why: Sudhan asked for direct address; research agrees.
- **The hook is a specific statement, never a yes or no question.**
- **One story structure per reel** (story/formats.json), not one of the last three used.
- **Every reel has a hook, explanation, payoff and a loop** back into the opening shot.
- **End with energy, then the address after a beat.** The ending calls the viewer to act with Floey ("Come on, let's go fix it!"), then a short pause, then "useflowra.com" on its own. Never end on a flat line that mixes the problem and the address. Why: Sudhan disliked "another page started fading... start free at useflowra.com" as one breath.
- **Animation explains, captions carry the words.** Never put the spoken sentence on screen as a text card; show it happening.

## Voice and sound
- **Professional reels use a narrator, not Floey's voice:** a clear, grown-up voice (Kokoro af_bella with af_heart, natural pitch), still louder than the music.
- **Floey has two approved voices** (brand/voice.json): "clean" (Kokoro) and "acted" (Chatterbox), both from the same af_heart-based Floey voice. Both are approved; choose per reel (acted for story-heavy reels). Every line carries an emotion that matches the moment. Why: Sudhan loved both and wants to choose by preference.
- **Every action has its own sound,** panned to where it happens. No silent actions.

## Design
- **Text never overflows its box.** Measure text; size boxes to it. Why: the end card overflowed in reel 000.
- **Keep the Flowra logo intact and recognisable.** The logo (the spark) and the character (Floey) are separate: never merge them, recolour the logo or hide it behind Floey. Why: people must know which is the logo and which is the character.
- **When Floey sits on the logo, she perches on the spark's left arm like a bird on a branch,** arriving sideways and facing the camera once seated. Never on the top point, never covering the spark. Why: Sudhan asked for it on the profile picture and the site nav.
- **Check every still at phone size before rendering the full reel.**

## Quality bar
- Sudhan reviews every reel before it posts. Aim for "too good", not "good enough": motion with anticipation and follow-through, small behaviours (blinks, preening, tongue flicks), and a story with a real ending.
