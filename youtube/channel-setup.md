# Create the Flowra YouTube channel

About 20 minutes. Files are in Desktop/Flowra/YouTube.

## 1. Pick the Google account
Use a Flowra Google account, not your personal one, ideally the one that already owns useflowra.com in Search Console. Sign in to youtube.com with it.

## 2. Create the channel as a brand channel
1. Click your profile picture (top right), then **Settings**.
2. Click **Add or manage your channel(s)**, then **Create a channel**.
3. Name: **Flowra**. Creating it here makes a brand channel, so you can add team members later and it isn't tied to your own name.
4. When it asks for a handle, choose **@useflowra** (if it's taken, try @flowrahq or @flowraseo).

## 3. Verify your phone (unlocks custom thumbnails and videos over 15 minutes)
1. Open **studio.youtube.com** (YouTube Studio).
2. **Settings** (bottom left), then **Channel**, then **Feature eligibility**.
3. Under "Features that require phone verification", click **Verify phone number** and enter the code.

## 4. Banner and profile picture
In YouTube Studio, go to **Customization**, then **Branding**:
- **Picture:** upload `profile-picture-1080.png`.
- **Banner image:** upload `channel-banner-2560x1440.png`. In the preview, check the headline shows on the "All devices" view. It's designed to.
- **Video watermark:** skip for now.
Click **Publish** (top right).

## 5. Channel details
Still in **Customization**, open **Basic info**:
- **Description:** paste the text below.
- **Links:** add three: Flowra → https://useflowra.com · Free SEO tools → https://useflowra.com/tools · Blog → https://useflowra.com/blog
- **Contact info:** support@useflowra.com
Click **Publish**.

Description:

> Flowra helps SEO teams and content agencies find the pages losing traffic, fix them and prove the fix worked.
>
> On this channel: short, practical tutorials on Google Search Console, content decay, content audits, AI Overviews and AI search traffic, and how to update old posts without losing rankings. Every video comes with a written guide on our blog.
>
> You'll also meet Floey, our hummingbird, who explains one SEO idea at a time in Shorts.
>
> New Shorts every Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and a longer tutorial every other week.
>
> Free to start: https://useflowra.com

## 6. Defaults for every upload
In **Settings**, then **Upload defaults**:
- **Description:** `Free to start: https://useflowra.com?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video`
- **Tags:** seo, google search console, content marketing, seo tutorial, content decay
- Under **Advanced settings**: Category **Science & Technology**, video language **English**.

## 7. Audience and keywords
In **Settings**, then **Channel**:
- **Basic info:** set your country. Keywords: `seo "google search console" "content marketing" "content decay" "seo tutorial" "ai overviews"`
- **Advanced settings:** "Do you want to set your channel as made for kids?" choose **No, set this channel as not made for kids**.
Click **Save**.

## 8. Tell Claude the channel link
Send the channel link (youtube.com/@useflowra). Claude then:
- posts every reel as a YouTube Short (by hand at first: Claude prepares the file and text, you click upload);
- sets up automatic uploads, which needs you to sign in once, plus Google's approval (a few weeks; until then, videos an app uploads are forced private);
- builds Tutorial 1 for your review on 19 October.
