## Signals
- **Duo sitcom reels hold Instagram viewers longer than every floey-garden or pro reel that had real views. This is now repeated across six reels.** (high). Average Instagram watch time for the duo reels: r011 9.6s of 25s (watchShare 0.39), r014 8.8s of 28s (0.31), r008 8.3s of 34s (0.25), r010 7.3s of 32s (0.23), r012 5.2s of 32s (0.16), r013 4.8s of 26s (0.18). Garden and pro reels: r002 4.2s, r006 3.0s, r007 2.7s, r003 2.2s. r004 shows 9.9s, but on only 5 views. The five reels with the most total watch time are all duo reels: r011 1,572s, r014 1,085s, r010 749s, r012 537s, r013 515s. The best garden reel, r002, has 370s.
- **Duo reels reliably reach about 100 or more people on Instagram. Garden reels were hit or miss.** (medium). Instagram views for the duo reels at 3.7 to 10.7 days: r011 193, r014 140, r013 118, r010 117, r012 113, r008 12. Garden reels at a similar age (day 2 to 4 snapshots): r006 115, r002 87, r003 15, r004 2. So five of six duo reels passed 100 views, against one of four garden reels.
- **Better attention has not turned into saves or shares. This is our biggest gap.** (medium). Across all 11 reels there are only 3 saves (r006 2, r012 1) and 2 shares (r006 1, r011 1). The five duo reels with a 'send' goal (r008, r010, r011, r013, r014) got 1 share from 505 combined reach. Saves only ever appeared on reels with a 'save' goal.
- **The first duo reel with a save goal (r012) earned a save, but fewer than r006 did, and it held people less than the other duo reels.** (low). r012 is a duo reel with a save goal, on a topic close to r006 (impressions up, clicks flat). At 5.6 days: 1 save and 0 shares from 101 reach. r006 had 2 saves and 1 share from 104 reach. r012 held 5.2s, near the bottom of the duo range (4.8 to 9.6s) but still above r006's 3.0s. This is one reel.
- **Length does not explain which duo reels hold people. Some short ones held well and some held poorly.** (low). Seconds watched by length: 25s held 9.6s (r011), 26s held 4.8s (r013), 28s held 8.8s (r014), 32s held 7.3s (r010) and 5.2s (r012), 34s held 8.3s (r008). Shorter reels get a higher watchShare almost by default. In seconds, there is no pattern. None of these reels was labeled as a length test.

'Animated action': r011 9.6s, r008 8.3s, but r008 got only 12 views. 'Voiced frustration': r014 8.8s, r012 5.2s. ## Do more
- Keep making duo sitcom reels with Maya. All six held people longer than any garden reel with real views, and five of six reached over 100 Instagram views.
- Open on Maya stuck in the middle of a concrete action with a personal stake, like r011 ('clicked Request Indexing forty times', 9.6s held) and r014 ('She'll think I did nothing', 8.8s held).
- Use the save goal on duo reels with a practical, reusable check. Saves have only ever come from save-goal reels (r006, r012).
- Label each new reel with the experiment it tests, so we can judge it after a few days.
- Judge Instagram at day 2 and YouTube at about day 10. That is when each one stops growing.

## Do less
- Floey-garden reels. They held people for only 2.2 to 4.2 seconds, and only one of four reached 100 Instagram views.
- The send goal without a clear ask. Five send reels got 1 share from 505 reach.
- Changing two things at once in a test, as r007 did with style and length.
- Treating r010's 506 YouTube views as normal. The other duo reels got 19 to 142.
- Chasing likes or reading much into them. They matter least to us, and they stay at 0 to 8 per reel.
