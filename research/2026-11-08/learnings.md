## Do more
- Keep making duo reels. They are our reliable way to reach about 100 to 200 people and hold them for 7 to 10 seconds.
- Keep using voiced frustration openings, where Maya complains in her own words. Five of six held people for 7.9s or more.
- Put the test name in the experiment field of every test reel, not only in the title, so we can track each test.
- Keep save goals on reels that teach a check people can repeat. Every save we have came from a save-goal reel.

## Do less
- Long garden explainers (36 to 42s). They held people for only 2 to 4 seconds and mostly reached few people.
- Counting on a send goal to produce shares on its own. Send-goal duo reels got 1 share from 755 reach.
- Judging a reel by its YouTube number alone. It swings from 4 to 1,113 views and we cannot explain why yet.
- Changing two things in one test, as we did with r007.
