## Do more
- Keep making duo (Maya and Floey) reels. They are the only style that reliably holds people and reaches about 100 or more.
- Open the hook on the feeling or the action, with no number as the first word. For example r033 'My best post is gone from Google' (9.8s) or r030 'My best post has last year's prices!' (9.3s).
- Lean on the two topics that produced saves: 'Google shows you but nobody clicks' and 'publishing more posts does not help'.
- Give viewers something to keep, like a check or steps they can use later, as r006 did. That is still our best reel for saves and shares.
- Put the test name in the experiment field on every reel made for a test, so we can tell after 3 days whether it worked.

## Do less
- Hooks whose first word is a number ('Thirty posts', 'Third for', 'One post'). Put the number later in the reel.
- More plain sitcom reels with a send goal and no clear reason to share. 12 of them have produced 1 share from 1,299 reach.
- Garden and pro reels for now. None held people past 4.2s with real views.
- Writing the test name only in the title or id. It cannot be tracked there.
- Judging reels on likes or YouTube spikes. Saves and shares are the numbers that lead to customers, and YouTube does not report them.
