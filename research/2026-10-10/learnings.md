## Signals
- **Viewers leave in the first few seconds on Instagram, so the hooks are not holding people yet.** (low). Average watch time was 3.9s of 38s on r002 (watchShare 0.10), 2.4s of 36s on r003 (0.07) and 0.7s of 42s on r006 (0.02). r004 shows 0.36, but that comes from only 2 Instagram views, so it tells us nothing. All four reels are 36 to 42 seconds long, so we cannot yet say whether length matters.
- **No reel has turned viewers into savers or sharers yet, even though all four aimed for saves.** (low). Instagram saves and shares are 0 on all four reels. The reel with the most Instagram views, r002 (87 views, 76 reach), got only 2 likes. YouTube likes and comments are 0 on all four.
- **The two platforms rank the reels in opposite order. The problem reel did best on Instagram, while the news reels did better on YouTube.** (low). Instagram views: r002 (problem) 87, r003 (news) 15, r006 (news) 6, r004 (news) 2. YouTube views: r004 117, r003 48, r006 18, r002 6. Each reel has only one snapshot, taken at a different age (0.5 to 3.6 days), so we cannot compare them at the same age. This updates last week's finding, which looked only at Instagram.
- **The hooks that led on each platform both opened with one specific, concrete detail.** (low). r002 led on Instagram with 'Search Console finds yours in a minute' (a named tool and a time frame). r004 led on YouTube with 'only fits Google's spam update if it started after September twenty fourth' (an exact date). The weaker hooks either warn ('likely hasn't recrawled it') or hedge ('may keep showing'). This rests on one reel per platform, and no hook pattern was recorded for any reel.
- **We have no Facebook numbers yet.** (low). The Facebook field is empty on all four reels, so Facebook plays, reach and watch time cannot be compared.

## Do more
- Open with one concrete detail: a named tool, a time frame, an exact date or a number.
- Look at Instagram and YouTube separately, since they ranked the same reels in opposite order.
- Write down the hook pattern for every reel so we can compare hooks properly.
- Take growth snapshots at fixed ages (day 1, day 3, day 7) so reels can be compared at the same age.
- Check why Facebook returns no data and fix it, so we have a third platform to read.

## Do less
- Hedged or warning-only hooks ('may', 'likely') that hold back the payoff.
- Spending the first seconds on setup and explanation, since most Instagram viewers are gone by second 1 to 4.
- Treating likes as a sign of success. They are tiny and matter least for us.
