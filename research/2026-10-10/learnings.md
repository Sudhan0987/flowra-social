- **Instagram viewers leave in the first few seconds, so the hooks are not holding people yet.** (low). Average Instagram watch time was 3.9s of 38s on r002 (watchShare 0.10), 2.4s of 36s on r003 (0.07) and 0.7s of 42s on r006 (0.02). r004 shows 0.36 (13.2s of 37s), but that comes from only 2 Instagram views, so it tells us nothing. All four reels are 36 to 42 seconds long, so we cannot yet say whether length matters.
- **No reel has turned viewers into savers or sharers yet, even though all four aimed for saves.** (low). Instagram saves and shares are 0 on all four reels. The reel with the most Instagram views, r002 (87 views, 76 reach), got only 2 likes. YouTube likes and comments are 0 on all four.
- **The two platforms rank the reels in opposite order. The problem reel leads on Instagram, while the news reels lead on YouTube.** (low). Instagram views: r002 (problem) 87, r003 (news) 15, r006 (news) 6, r004 (news) 2. YouTube views: r004 116, r003 49, r006 17, r002 6. Since last week, YouTube moved by one view on three reels (r003 48 to 49, r004 117 to 116, r006 18 to 17), which is noise. Each reel still has only one snapshot, taken at a different age (0.5 to 3.6 days), so we cannot compare them at the same age. As a rough check, r004 has about 50 YouTube views per day of age and r002 under 2, so the gap is too big to be only about age.
- **The hooks that led on each platform both opened with one specific, concrete detail.** (low). r002 led on Instagram with 'Search Console finds yours in a minute' (a named tool and a time frame). r004 led on YouTube with 'only fits Google's spam update if it started after September twenty fourth' (an exact date), in the explain-like-five format. The weaker hooks either warn ('likely hasn't recrawled it') or hedge ('may keep showing'). This rests on one reel per platform, every reel used a different format, and no hook pattern was recorded for any reel.
- **We still have no Facebook numbers.** (low). The Facebook field is empty on all four reels, so we cannot compare Facebook plays, reach or watch time.

## Do less
- Hedging or warning hooks with no concrete payoff, like 'may keep showing' (r006, watchShare 0.02) or 'likely hasn't recrawled it' (r003, 0.07).
- Making all reels 36 to 42 seconds long. Viewers leave after 1 to 4 seconds on Instagram, and with no shorter reels we cannot test length.
- Reading the platform results as one number. Instagram and YouTube rank our reels in opposite order, so judge each separately.
