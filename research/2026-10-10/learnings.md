We have four reels and very small audiences, so nothing here is a rule yet. ## Signals
- **Viewers leave in the first few seconds, so the hooks are not holding people yet.** (low). Average watch time was 3.9s of 38s on r002 (watchShare 0.10), 2.4s of 36s on r003 (0.07) and 0.7s of 42s on r006 (0.02). r004 shows 0.36, but that comes from only 2 views, so it tells us nothing.
- **No reel has turned viewers into savers or sharers yet, even though all four aimed for saves.** (low). Saves and shares are 0 on all four reels. The most seen reel, r002 (87 views, 76 reach), got only 2 likes. Follows and profile visits are missing from the data, so we cannot judge the numbers that matter most.
- **The one problem reel reached far more people than the three news reels, but it is also the oldest, so this may just be extra time.** (low). r002 (problem pillar) had 87 views at 3.6 days. The news reels had 15 views at 2.6 days (r003), 2 at 2.3 days (r004) and 6 at 0.5 days (r006). Each reel has only one growth snapshot, so we cannot compare them at the same age.
- **The hook that reached the most people promised a concrete result with a named tool and a time frame.** (low). r002: 'Search Console finds yours in a minute.' The other hooks either warn ('Google likely hasn't recrawled it') or hedge ('may keep showing'). This rests on one reel only, and no hook pattern was recorded for any reel.

## Do more
- Hooks that promise a concrete result in a set time, like r002's 'finds yours in a minute'
- Problem reels about a pain the viewer already feels (keep testing this, it is not proven)
- Record the hook pattern for every reel so we can compare hooks
- Take a growth snapshot for every reel each day so we can compare them at the same age
- Pull follows, profile visits and Facebook numbers, which are empty right now

## Do less
- Slow openings that spend the first seconds setting up instead of showing the payoff
- Hedged hooks built on 'may' or 'likely' that give viewers no clear reason to stay
- Leaning almost only on news reels (3 of 4) before we know they work

## Try next
- Make the next two reels 20 to 25 seconds long, and show the payoff (the screen, the number or the result) in the first 2 seconds, before any explanation. Why: People leave after 1 to 4 seconds on 36 to 42 second reels. A shorter reel that pays off sooner should hold more of them. Measure: Instagram average watch time and watchShare, compared with the current 0.02 to 0.10.
- Post one problem reel and one news reel in the same week, both opening with a concrete promise hook, and compare them on the same day of their life. Why: The problem reel did best, but it was also the oldest. A matched test separates the topic from the extra time. Measure: Instagram views and reach at day 1 and day 3 from the growth snapshots, then saves, follows and profile visits.
- On one save goal reel, show a simple on-screen checklist and say plainly 'save this for your next audit' near the end. Why: All four reels aimed for saves and got zero. Viewers may need a clear thing worth keeping and a direct ask. Measure: Instagram saves, compared with 0 on every reel so far.
