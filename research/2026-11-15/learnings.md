The best garden reel, r002, has 370s.
- **Duo reels reliably reach 100 to 200 people on Instagram. Garden reels were hit or miss.** (high). Instagram views for the duo reels: r011 193, r024 150, r017 148, r014 141, r028 139, r015 136, r026 126, r019 122, r013 118, r016 118, r010 117, r012 113. The one miss is r008 with 12. Garden reels: r006 117, r002 89, r003 18, r004 5. Pro reel r007: 15. So 12 of 13 duo reels passed 100, against 1 of 4 garden reels.
- **Better attention still has not turned into saves or shares. This is our biggest gap. The idea that saves only come from save-goal reels no longer holds.** (medium). Across 18 reels there are 5 saves (r006 2, r012 1, r017 1, r028 1) and 2 shares (r006 1, r011 1). The 8 duo reels with a send goal got 1 share and 1 save from 880 combined reach. The 5 duo reels with a save goal (r012, r016, r017, r019, r026) got 2 saves and 0 shares from 571 reach. Garden reel r006 alone got 2 saves and 1 share from 104 reach. r028 has a send goal and still got a save.

## Do less

- Opening the hook with a number as the first word, at least until we have tested it.
- Stretching duo reels past 32 seconds without a reason.
- Garden and pro reels made the old way. They held people for 2 to 4 seconds.
- Reading much into likes or a single YouTube spike.
