# Audits (run all five on every duo draft)

## 1. Flip audit (the most important)
- Cover Floey's lines and read Maya's hook as a stranger: what would you think? That must be THE INSTINCT.
- Once Floey flips it, does the viewer think "oh, of course"? If they think "that's not true", check the source or drop it.
- The tip must work if the viewer does it today, exactly as said.

## 2. Clarity audit
- Words a 12 year old knows; one name per thing ("the Pages report", every time).
- Each line is one thought and one picture. Two thoughts in one line: split it.

## 3. Speed audit
- Maya speaks at 0.3 s; the problem is said by second 2; the flip by about second 5.
- 50-75 words, 6-12 lines. No line that only repeats another. The button is the last spoken line.

## 4. Voice audit (Kokoro realities)
- Numbers as words in `say`; digits only in `caption` or on screen props.
- No bare letters ("plan B"), no capitals, no symbols, no URLs.
- A key word with a sound-alike twin goes where context fixes it.
- Very short lines (1-2 words) need a pause after them (0.3+) or they blur into the next.

## 5. Character and truth audit
- Each line sounds like its speaker (`brand/cast/README.md`): Maya dramatic but never dumb; Floey dry, brief, never selling.
- Every fact is in the brief with a source. Numbers Maya panics about are hers (examples), never claimed as industry data.
- No real person, brand or competitor as the joke. No Flowra in the dialogue.
