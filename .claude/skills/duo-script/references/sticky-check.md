# Stickiness check (SUCCESs) for Maya & Floey scripts

Score each trait 1-10; total 6-60. Bands: 50-60 extremely sticky · 42-49 strong · 30-41 forgettable · below 30 rewrite.
**Target: 48+, no trait below 7.** Report the score, the two lowest traits and the fix for each.

| Trait | Question for a duo reel | Signs it's weak | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | Can the flip be said in one sentence a friend would repeat? | two tips; a tip that needs a table | One flip, one tip. Cut every line that is not setup, flip, tip, check or button. |
| **Unexpected** | Would half the viewers share Maya's instinct, then be surprised? | the answer is obvious from the hook | Pick a sharper instinct; let Maya's reaction sell the surprise; hold a beat before the reveal. |
| **Concrete** | Can every line be acted and drawn? | "your metrics", "SEO stuff" | Real names (Search Console, Sunday), one number, one action shown on her laptop. |
| **Credible** | Would a viewer trust the tip? | no source; a claim about Google from memory | A Google doc or a major industry source in the brief; the check shown on screen. |
| **Emotional** | Do we feel Maya's panic, then relief? | calm, flat Maya; no reaction | A real panic in the hook, a visible reaction on the flip, a button that releases the tension. |
| **Story** | Does something change from line to line? | a Q and A list | but/so chain, a doubt line, a check that turns it, a button that answers the hook. |
