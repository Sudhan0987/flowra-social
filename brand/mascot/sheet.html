Hovering, saying hello
Design notes
  • Spectrum throat (gorget): real hummingbird throats change colour with angle. Ours sweeps the Flowra spectrum on "found it" and "it worked" moments.
  • Spark catchlight: the shine in the eye is the Flowra logo star.
  • Shorter, softer beak than a real bird: friendlier, and it opens to talk and points at things.
  • Crest feathers carry mood: up when alert or excited, flat when worried.
  • Wings: a figure-8 blur when hovering (real wings beat 50 to 80 times a second, faster than video can show), and a feather "hand" when it needs to point or wave.
  • Tail fans out to brake after a dart.
