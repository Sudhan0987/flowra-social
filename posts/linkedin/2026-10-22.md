# SEO report: lead with the CTR drop, not the 18%

Impressions up 18%, clicks up 6%, CTR down from 3.0% to 2.7%.

That is one sample month from our new guide to SEO reports, and the 18% is the line most summaries would lead with.

The slides go through why the 2.7% deserves the first sentence instead, and how we order a monthly report so nothing in it catches the reader off guard.

A report full of numbers and no actions leaves the reader asking "so what?", and that reader is often the one paying for the work. If a client finds the CTR drop before you mention it, you end up explaining it on their terms.

Which line in your monthly report gets the most questions from clients or your boss?
