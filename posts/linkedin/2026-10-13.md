# A three step client script for AI Overview traffic drops

39 comments this week under one r/SEO thread asking how to explain to clients that traffic dropped because of AI Overviews.

We read through them, and the replies kept landing on the same move: show the client the numbers you are looking at yourself.

We turned it into a three step script for the next call where the chart points down and someone wants a name for it.

1. Open Search Console together and pull the past month of queries. Go through the ones that lost clicks so the client sees them in their own data first. People trust a drop they spotted themselves more than one you describe to them.

2. Name the cause once, plainly. On many informational searches Google now answers on the results page with an AI Overview, and some people never need to click. Then move on, because repeating it is where it starts to sound like a defense.

3. Put conversions next to traffic, using the same date range for both so nobody can say you picked a flattering window. Commenters said conversions have often held steady while visits fell. If that holds for this client, it is the number that matters most in the meeting. If it does not, you have a different problem, and it is better to find it on this call.

Get the order right. Open with AI Overviews and it sounds like an excuse. Start with their own queries and by the time you name the cause, they have already seen it.

The query review feels slow while you are in it. It is also the part that makes the explanation believable when you get to it.
