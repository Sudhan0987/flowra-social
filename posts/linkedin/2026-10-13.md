# AI Overviews traffic drop: a 3 step client call script

A company sued its web developer over a traffic drop. In a story shared on r/SEO, the expert witness traced the cause to Google's AI Overviews, not the developer.

Few of us will end up in court. But most agencies have heard some version of that accusation on a client call.

The advice in the thread was simple: show the client the same numbers you are looking at. Here it is as a three step script.

1. Open Search Console and pull the queries from the past month. Point out the ones that still earn impressions but send fewer clicks. That gap is where the AI Overviews conversation starts.

2. Put conversions next to it. Commenters noted that conversions have often held steady while traffic fell. If that is true for this client, say it plainly: fewer visits, same business.

3. Agree what you will both watch next month. Name the queries, the pages and the conversion numbers, so the next call starts from shared data instead of a feeling.

The goal is not to win the argument. It is to stop anyone blaming the wrong cause, whether that is the developer or you.
