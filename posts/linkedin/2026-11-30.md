# Skipped service pages vs indexed ones: compare side by side

Google indexed every service page on the site except the ones the business actually needs.

That is the situation an r/TechSEO poster described this week. They submitted the URLs again and again. A few service pages built on nearly the same layout still stay out.

The skipped pages share a site, a template and a resubmission history with the indexed ones. So the first thing we'd check is how much of each skipped page would still read true on its sibling.

We already covered pressing Request Indexing and reading the reason line. This is the step after that.

Open one skipped page and one indexed page side by side. Mark every block you could paste onto the other with only the service name changed:

- the intro paragraph
- the process steps
- the FAQ
- the call to action

What stays unmarked is the page's reason to exist on its own. If that is one heading, another resubmit won't change much.

So give each skipped page something only it says:

- the questions customers ask about that one service, in their words
- how long it takes, from first call to finished job
- a job you did on that service, with what was asked and what you delivered
- the area it covers, if it differs from your other pages

Start with one pair rather than the whole set. That way a change in status points back to one rewrite.

Then resubmit once and give it time. Resubmitting an unchanged page only repeats what the poster already tried.

Write down the date you change those pages. Flowra's Index Coverage Monitor records when a page's index status changes, so you can see if the rewrite moved it.

When you put a skipped page next to an indexed one on your sites, what is usually the difference?
