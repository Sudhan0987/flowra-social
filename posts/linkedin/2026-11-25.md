# duo-countdown: ranking held, clicks fell, three checks

Position 1 on desktop got 17.83% of clicks in September 2025. By August 2026 it got 7.43%.

Same spot, less than half the clicks (DataForSEO, 2,000+ sites). So "my position didn't move and my clicks still fell" may have nothing to do with your page.

It shows up in Search Console as a familiar chart: the impressions line holds steady while the clicks line slides away underneath it.

Three checks in Search Console before you rewrite anything:

3. Did the position move? Open Performance and compare the weeks before the drop with the weeks after. If position and impressions held and only CTR fell, the change happened above your listing on the results page.

2. Is it one page or every page? Filter by page before you look at queries. One page points to that query. Every page points to the results page itself: AI Overviews appeared on 5.93% of results in September 2024 and 61.43% in August 2026.

1. Did it start the same week everywhere? Line up the drop date across pages, then split by device. Desktop position 1 CTR fell by nearly half in March 2026 and stayed low, while mobile mostly recovered by May. A drop that starts the same week across many pages points outside your content.

If all three point outside the page, put that in writing before anyone books a rewrite.

Reddit is seeing a version of this. Similarweb puts its October visits down 13% on a year earlier. Reddit disputes the method, and its CEO has tied weaker Google referrals to AI Mode and AI Overviews.

The CTR data doesn't prove cause, and its desktop sample shrank by about a third. We still think rewriting a page that holds its rank is an expensive fix for a change on someone else's results page.

Which of the three checks has caught a false alarm for you?
