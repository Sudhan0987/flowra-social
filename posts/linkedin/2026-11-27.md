# Traffic drop: every page or a few?

4 suspects come up every time a site with strong backlinks keeps losing organic traffic: technical, content, a Google update or the links.

Someone in r/bigseo asked this week which of those to check first.

Our answer is none of them, yet. Open Search Console, compare the last 28 days with the 28 before and sort the Pages tab by clicks lost.

Every page slides in the same window: the cause is sitewide. Check:
• update dates
• a template or robots.txt change
• a migration
• your tracking

A handful of pages slide: the cause lives on those pages. Check for:
• stale content
• a competitor's better answer
• two of your URLs splitting one query
• a page that fell out of the index

Domain and backlink profile are identical in both cases. The only variable is how many pages fell, and it sends you to a different set of checks.

The costly version is a week in a backlink tool while the cause is one page Google stopped indexing. Links still get checked, later, on the pages the split points to.

If the site is seasonal, compare against the same weeks last year. Search Console keeps 16 months of data.

When traffic slid on a site you run, was it every page or a few, and what was it in the end?
