# Hello, LinkedIn. This is Flowra (first post)

Hello, LinkedIn. This is Flowra.

Most blogs don't have a traffic problem. They have a forgetting problem.

A post ranks, brings in clicks for a year, then slowly slips. The site total looks flat because new posts cover the gap, so nobody notices until a page that used to matter has lost half its clicks.

We built Flowra to catch that and fix it:

1. Find. It connects to Google Search Console and GA4 and flags the pages losing traffic, plus the ones sitting just off page one.

2. Fix. For each page it reads the results ranking above it and plans what to change, section by section. Then it rewrites only the parts that need it, in your own writing style, and keeps what already works.

3. Publish and prove. The update goes to WordPress once you approve it, and Flowra measures the result against the rest of your site, so a good month for everyone isn't counted as your fix working.

It's made for SEO teams and content agencies who would rather fix the pages they already have than keep publishing new ones into the void.
