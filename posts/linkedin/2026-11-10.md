# 45 of 48 clicks: ask the site that works

45 of 48 Google clicks in a month came from one site out of five.

That's a solo builder on r/juststart this week (thread in the first comment). Five new tool sites, and the question underneath: how does a new domain pick keywords it can actually rank for? It was the busiest SEO thread in our listening this month, 14 comments in.

The usual answer is a volume database and a difficulty score. We'd start somewhere cheaper.

The one site that works has already told you what Google is willing to rank it for:

- Filter Search Console by that page.
- Read every query it wins, the small ones too.
- Build the next page around the pattern they share.

For a team running several young sites or client domains, that list is the most honest keyword research you have, because it's what Google already agreed to.

The carousel has the four steps, including the band of queries sitting just off page one.

When a new site gets its first clicks, do you plan the next page from a keyword tool or from its own queries?
