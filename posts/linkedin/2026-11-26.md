# send-ask: Site move step zero, list click-earning pages before redirects

Close to half of the store's revenue is organic, and every category URL is about to change.

That was the setup in an r/TechSEO thread this week. The boss wants the new platform with no traffic drop at all.

Nobody can promise zero. What a team can control is the order of the work.

The short version, before anyone writes a redirect:
- Export clicks per page from Search Console and sort by them.
- The pages that earn get mapped first and checked by a person.
- Everything else comes after.

The slides walk through the four steps, plus the two shortcuts that skip them.

Which page on your site would you map by hand, whatever the spreadsheet says?
