# New site missing from its own name search

104 impressions and 1 click within 24 hours of adding a new site to Search Console. That was a post in r/TechSEO this week.

Same week, r/bigseo: a beginner searched their brand new site's exact name and Google showed everyone but them.

For that second site, the first thing we'd check is whether it's in Search Console at all, before anyone touches the content.

The slides are the order we'd follow on any new domain, including a client's:

1. Verify the property.
2. Submit the sitemap.
3. Then leave Request Indexing alone.

Google's own docs say requesting a recrawl of the same URL again and again won't get it crawled any faster, and that crawling can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks.

What do you check first when a new site won't show up for its own name?

P.S. Google's recrawl guidance is in the first comment.
