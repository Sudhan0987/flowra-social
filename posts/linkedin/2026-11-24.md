# AI visits vs Google: check the ratio in your own GA4

Almost 1 to 1. That is how one r/juststart post title put AI traffic against Google on a single store.

The post was removed, so we treat it as a claim. One store is also not your site.

Your own GA4 can settle it for your pages. The slides go one step at a time:

1. The report to open
2. The AI referrers to filter
3. What to set next to them
4. How to read the ratio month by month instead of off one good week

One limit before you swipe. GA4 only sees visits that arrive. When an assistant answers a question and nobody clicks, no analytics tool records it, so treat your ratio as a floor for AI's influence.

Flowra reads the same GA4 referrers and shows them next to Search Console clicks, if you would rather not rebuild the filter every month.

What ratio did you get, and over how many months?
